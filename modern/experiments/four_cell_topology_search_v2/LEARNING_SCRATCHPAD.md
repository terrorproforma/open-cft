## 2026-09-02 preregistration

- [user] Physical mirror evidence requires connected constant-psi surfaces,
  three independent maps, atomic quantiles, complete uncertainty, and an
  explicit electron adiabaticity gate.
- [audit] Accepted coupling v3 already fails closed on open/disconnected
  contours, exact or near-null segment bounds, uncertainty-dominated extrema,
  missing electron inputs, nonadiabatic rho_e/L_B, and unstable three-map
  topology.
- [self] Coupling v3 registers four field-defined cells/cusps, while the
  topology-general N=4 plasma chain has three interior edges plus one anode
  boundary. The preregistered mapping therefore uses the first three bounded
  cell distributions as ordered interior edges and the fourth as anode arrival.
- [self] A cell-level interval must preserve the full quantile/component
  distribution. Its lower and upper values are the envelope across every
  accepted surface, while its nominal is the arithmetic mean fixed before run.
- [self] Shared field uncertainty induces shared probability uncertainty. The
  plasma topology carries fully positive cross-cell covariance rather than
  silently assuming independent errors.
- [test] The initial stack-start range permitted a thick first magnet to cross
  z=0. Before preregistration, the lower bound was raised from 1.6 mm to 2.8 mm;
  all 128 rounded candidates then passed strict geometry and paired-source
  construction.
- [test] A 1.2 mm equivalent-current smear was narrower than two radial cells
  in one preregistered lower-resolution domain. Before preregistration it was
  raised to 1.5 mm, after which every candidate/role passed the accepted source
  support contract.
- [test] Synthetic v3 preflight confirmed closed constant-psi tracing, rejection
  of equal-z/different-psi samples, and fail-closed exact-saddle handling.
- [policy] N=4 is structurally rank 22 for 25 state variables. The search uses
  the full-rank publication policy, so even a strict residual root cannot emit
  a state vector, power balance, or performance claim.
