# CFT topology characterization v1

This preregistered developmental study measures the physical relationship among
magnet stage count, clustered vector nulls, X-type cell-bounding cusps, and
O-type closed-flux cells. It is not a design optimization or blind validation.

The fixed 56-case Cartesian family covers 2–8 stages, two pitches, two chamber
radii, and both first polarities. Every case receives independent primary,
refined, and enlarged-domain L1a solves. Root counts are not compared until one
mesh-scaled 2D clustering policy and global cross-map assignment have run.

Eligibility is physical and fail-closed: a cusp must be an interior channel
vector null with converged X Jacobian/index and offset constant-psi evidence of
a cell-bounding separatrix. Hardware, wall, axial-exterior, and finite-box roots
remain in the dataset with explicit exclusion reasons.

No mirror probability, plasma state, power, or performance is computed. Results
may inform only a later, separately preregistered search v3.
