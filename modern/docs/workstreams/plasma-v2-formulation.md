## 1. Where v2 starts

The v1 ledger (`spec/plasma/equation-ledger.json`, rows R00-R27) has no
admissible root for any positive interior cusp probability: on the R00-R26
manifold the global row reduces to
`R27 = 2 (j_e3 (1-p4) + I4)(phi_4 - Ua) + EI (p1 j_e0 + p2 j_e1 + p3 j_e2)`
(`docs/workstreams/global-plasma-closure-analysis.md`, section 4). The ledger
records two corrections as `PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED`:

1. `Pcusp' = sum_k j_e,k-1 p_k dE_k` - drop the `+EI` per cusp-lost electron,
   because the ionization energy is already booked once in `PI = EI sum I_k`
   (Kornfeld assumption 8 books it again as "recombination at boundaries").
2. `Panode_e' = j_e3 p4 (Ua - phi_3 + T3) + (I4 + j_e3 (1-p4))(Ua - phi_4 + T4)`
   - the electron sign for electrons arriving at the anode.

v2 applies both. The analysis document's prediction is confirmed
numerically: on 200 seeded manifold states the corrected R27 is zero to
4.8e-15 relative (v1's R27 on the same states is at least 5.5e-4 relative
and positive), and the scaled Jacobian of the corrected 28-row core has rank
21 on 25 unknowns (nullity 4: all four cell potentials are free). The
corrections alone therefore turn a non-closing model into an underdetermined
one, which is why they were not accepted without closure rows. v2 adds them.

### 2.1 Energy bookkeeping at a cusp

The corrected R27 books `L_k dE_k` per cusp (`L_k = j_e,k-1 p_k`,
`dE_k = phi_k - phi_{k-1} + T_{k-1}`), exactly Kornfeld's electronic + ionic
terms with the cusp potential cancelled and recombination booked once in
`PI`. The sheath rows identify the split without changing the total:
electron kinetic energy at the wall `L_k (dE_k - Dphi_s,k)`, ion fall
`L_k Dphi_s,k`. The review's Maxwellian forms (`2T_k + Dphi` per electron,
`Dphi + T_k/2` per ion) are reported as diagnostics only: booking them would
break the R27 identity because the cascade rows R23-R26 carry no presheath
term and the lost electrons are the monoenergetic entering beam.

**Consistency margin.** An electron that reaches the wall must arrive with
non-negative energy: `dE_k - Dphi_s,k >= 0`. On the manifold
`T_k (Je_k + I_k) = CT Je_k dE_k`, so the margin equals
`dE_k [1 - c_s CT Je_k/(Je_k + I_k)]` and its sign does not depend on the
step size:

    margin_k >= 0   <=>   c_s,k <= (1 + I_k/Je_k) / CT.

With `CT = 0.68`: the space-charge-limited sheath (`1.02 < 1/CT = 1.47`)
always passes; the no-emission floating sheath (`5.27`) passes only where
`I_k/Je_k >= CT ln K0 - 1 = 2.59`, i.e. `dE_k >= (EI/CI)(CT ln K0 - 1) = 447 V`
- the exit drop at `Ua >= 500 V` and nowhere else. This is verified exactly
in `tests/plasma_v2/test_sheath_rows.py`. The margin is enforced fail-closed
by default (`SolverPolicy.enforce_cusp_energy_margin`), so the Maxwellian
floating sheath without emission is *inconsistent with Kornfeld's
monoenergetic cascade* and the solver refuses to publish such states; the
record reports them as `manifold_root_blocked_by_cusp_energy_margin`.

The same logic bounds the anode: R31 plus the corrected anode electron term
(`Ua - phi_4 + T4 >= 0`) admits only anode ion fractions
`(-j_i4)/j_e4 <= e/K0 = 1.39 %`.

## 3. Rank: what is solved and what is declared

`rank_report` (pivoted Gram-Schmidt on the variable-scaled Jacobian at the
manifold projection of a state), at the Kornfeld DM9.2 point:

| block | rows x unknowns | rank | nullity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| corrected v1 core R00-R27 | 28 x 25 | 21 | 4 (all potentials) |
| + sheath R28-R30, anode R31, cusp-loss R35-R37 | 35 x 31 | 28 | 3 |
| + declared potential rows R32-R34 | 38 x 31 | 31 | 0 |

The sheath rows do **not** restore rank for the potentials: the density
cancels in the ambipolar balance, so each of R28-R30 identifies only its own
`Dphi_s,k` and says nothing about the cell-to-cell potentials. R31
identifies one potential relation. The remaining three are declared
(`CL-3-potentials`); the literature closes them by an electron cross-field
transport row that needs densities and B, which this model does not have.
Modes:

* **A (default)**: R31 sheath + R34 anode fall declared (0 V) -> `phi_1` SOLVED.
* **B**: R31 sheath + R34 cathode coupling declared -> anode fall SOLVED.
  The anode row has two roots for `V_c` just below the mode-A value and none
  admissible above it (the exit drop feeds back on the cascade,
  `d j_e4/d phi_2 ~ I_2/dE_2`, and the only remaining root has
  `phi_4 - Ua > T4`); at 1 kV/1 A the admissible band is `V_c <= 14.074 V`
  (14.0 V -> 13.2 V fall; 14.05 V -> 3.9 V; 14.1 V -> fail-closed).
* **C**: R31 declared fall + R34 cathode coupling -> all potentials declared
  (used to evaluate published states).

## 4. Solver

`solve_sheath_closure_multistart` reuses `cft_revival.plasma.
solve_bounded_least_squares` (variable-scaled column-pivoted QR
Levenberg-Marquardt) and `project_nondecreasing`, read-only. Starts: a
deterministic reduced solve on the R00-R30 manifold (v1
`potential_parametrized_state`, bracket scan + bisection on R31, CL-4
fixed point in `p`) plus the v1 five-pattern spread. Publication is
fail-closed as v1 (every normalized row <= 1e-9, finite, in the box, every
inequality margin >= 0); otherwise the floor and reason are reported and no
state is published. Two identical calls give identical vectors
(`test_solver_is_deterministic`). Typical cost: 0.2-2 s per multistart
solve in pure Python.

  through the cascade, `phi_1` - which lands at 14.07 V for Kornfeld's DM9.2

  the rounding of his table.
* The Maxwellian floating sheath without emission is inconsistent with the
  monoenergetic cascade at every cusp with `dE_k < 447 V` (closed-form
  criterion); only a near-space-charge-limited sheath (high-emission
  dielectric) is consistent with Kornfeld's cell rows. Kornfeld's own cusp
  potentials (0.4-0.8 T_k) sit below even the SCL value.
* Under the SCL sheath and CL-3, the effective loss probability is
  `0.36 A_k` (0.09-0.36 for the screening range) and the model closes
  everywhere except at 150 V with `A >= 0.87`, where the cascade cannot
  carry the anode current or the perveance law over-emits.
* CL-4 with segment-mean densities is not usable; it needs a cusp
  sheath-edge density.

Does not show: any thruster performance; any validation of the PIC or by the
PIC; that the flat-interior potential closure is right (the PIC says
otherwise); that `A_k` is a cusp probability (it is a collisionless access
fraction); that the corrections are accepted (the ledger entry stays
`PROPOSED_NOT_ACCEPTED`; acceptance would need re-admission under a new
analytic-consistency manifest).

## 7. Limitations and next steps

* Cell-to-cell steps are declared. The next identifying row is an electron
  cross-field transport relation across each cusp (anomalous
  `D_perp = 0.4 T/(eB)` as in the Greifswald PIC, or a PIC-declared
  conductance), which needs `n_k` and `B_k` - the same inputs CL-4 needs.
* The sheath temperature is the cell temperature `T_k`; the PIC shows a 6 eV
  near-wall population under a 40 eV axis, so a two-temperature cusp model
  (or the entering-beam energy) is the obvious refinement, and it would
  change the energy criterion of section 2.1.
* No neutral balance, prescribed anode current, Kornfeld's 1 A-class
  perveance (sub-volt plume potentials at mA currents) - all inherited from v1.
* Re-admission path: a new `analytic-consistency` manifest binding
  `plasma_v2`, this document and the record, with Kornfeld Table 3.1 as the
  reproduction target - only after the potential closure is either derived
  or explicitly accepted as a declaration.
