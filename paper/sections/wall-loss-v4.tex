\subsection{Collisionless full-orbit electron wall loss in the divergent-exit field}
\label{sec:wall-loss-v4}

\paragraph{Method.}
The campaign integrates relativistic Boris test-particle electron orbits in the
prescribed magnetostatic field of the \texttt{\WlfPTwoDesign} design, the third-level
P2 finite-element reference that carries the \texttt{\WlfPTwoQualification} status
(manifest hash prefix \texttt{\WlfPTwoManifestSha}, evidence commit prefix
\texttt{\WlfPTwoCommit}). Three hash-bound field maps are used
(\WlfMapRoleList): the bound quadratic $A_\phi$ solution is sampled onto a regular
$\psi$ grid (\WlfPrimaryGrid$\times$\WlfPrimaryGridAxial{} nodes for the primary and
enlarged-domain maps, \WlfRefinedGrid$\times$\WlfRefinedGridAxial{} for the refined
map) inside the homogeneous plasma sub-domain (regions \WlfPlasmaRegions), with a
withheld mid-cell adapter error of \WlfPrimaryAdapterRms{} relative RMS against a
limit of \WlfAdapterRmsLimit. Each of the \WlfCaseCount{} cases (map role $\times$
timestep policy \WlfPolicyList, with maximum gyro-rotations of \WlfPolicyN,
\WlfPolicyTwoN{} and \WlfPolicyFourN{} rad per step) launches \WlfLaunchesPerCase{}
electrons with equal weights: \WlfStrataPerCase{} strata formed by four axial cells
at $z = \WlfCellOneZMm$, \WlfCellTwoZMm, \WlfCellThreeZMm{} and \WlfCellFourZMm{} mm,
two kinetic energies (\WlfEnergies{} eV), two pitch angles (\WlfPitches$^\circ$) and
both parallel directions; each stratum holds \WlfRepeatsPerStratum{} launch radii
(\WlfRadiusInnerMm{} and \WlfRadiusOuterMm{} mm) times \WlfGyrophaseCount{}
gyrophases. The wall estimand is a hit on the straight cylindrical dielectric
($r = \WlfWallRadiusMm$ mm, $\WlfWallZMinMm \le z \le \WlfWallZMaxMm$ mm); radial exit
in the divergent section ($\WlfWallZMaxMm$--$\WlfDomainZMaxMm$ mm) and axial exit
through either plane are domain escapes, not wall events. Orbits are limited to
\WlfMaxTimeS{} s, \WlfMaxPathMm{} mm and \WlfMaxSteps{} steps; no orbit reached a
limit. The estimator is \texttt{\WlfEstimatorPolicy} with \texttt{\WlfIntervalMethod}
intervals. The launch cells are protocol positions in this one qualified field; they
are not demonstrated confinement cells.

The protocol was frozen before any outcome was observed and executed exactly
\WlfAttemptCount{} time with \texttt{orbit\_mc} \WlfOrbitMcVersion. Its identities are
the preregistration commit \texttt{\WlfPreregCommit}, the results commit
\texttt{\WlfResultsCommit}, the integrator source hash \texttt{\WlfOrbitMcSourceSha}
and the protocol hash \texttt{\WlfProtocolSha} (all quoted as prefixes; the full
digests are in the evidence file). Three earlier preregistered attempts terminated on
code defects before producing any orbit outcome (\texttt{\WlfVOneState}:
``\WlfVOneError''; \texttt{\WlfVTwoState}: ``\WlfVTwoError''; \texttt{\WlfVThreeState}:
``\WlfVThreeError''). This campaign therefore added a disclosed non-evidentiary
shakedown of the complete production path on the real fields
(\WlfShakedownLaunches{} launches on a design proven disjoint from the evidentiary
one: disjoint \WlfShakedownDisjoint, passed \WlfShakedownPassed, evidentiary
\WlfShakedownEvidentiary) that must pass before the preregistration can be frozen.
Acceptance is decided by \WlfGateCount{} binding gates: probability convergence in
timestep and across maps, a relative energy gate of \WlfEnergyGate, wall-endpoint
error, earliest-event ordering, the runtime rotation bound, manufactured convergence
orders, CPU/CUDA parity, material quarantine, exact authority replay, and zero
incomplete or numerical failures. A post-hoc audit of the sealed bundle found one
recording-layer defect, \WlfToleratedSidecars{} hash sidecars written with platform
line endings, and showed that every orbit artifact and every content hash is intact;
the evidence generator tolerates exactly that defect and nothing else.
