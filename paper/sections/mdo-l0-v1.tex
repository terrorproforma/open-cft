\subsection{Robust optimiser comparison on the operating-point model under declared cusp-probability uncertainty}
\label{sec:mdo-l0-v1}

\paragraph{Method.}
The campaign optimises the accepted operating-point model (fidelity
\texttt{\MdoFidelity}, Python reference backend) over \MdoDesignVariableCount{}
design variables: the discharge voltage from \MdoUaLower{} to \MdoUaUpper{} V, the
anode current from \MdoIaLower{} to \MdoIaUpper{} A and the propellant mass flow from
\MdoMdotLower{} to \MdoMdotUpper{} kg/s, that is the legacy operating variables
clipped to the accepted sweep domain. The \MdoExcludedVariableCount{} legacy
geometry radii are excluded because no geometry-to-model map survives the audit:
the legacy finite-element, cusp-window and mirror-formula chain is quarantined and
its mirror step is not supported by the admitted wall-loss campaign
(Section~\ref{sec:wall-loss-v4}); the exclusion is recorded as a structural finding
of the campaign. Its \MdoUncertainInputCount{} uncertain inputs carry independent
uniform priors: the per-cell cusp probabilities $p_k$ of the \MdoCellCount{} cells
on $[\MdoCuspLower, \MdoCuspUpper]$, the produced ionised fraction on
$[\MdoEtaLower, \MdoEtaUpper]$, the doubly charged ion share on
$[\MdoZetaLower, \MdoZetaUpper]$ and the divergence factor on
$[\MdoGammaLower, \MdoGammaUpper]$. The cusp prior is wide by design: its implied
mean survival, \MdoImpliedPriorSurvival, matches the pooled electron survival
\MdoVFourSurvival{} of the wall-loss campaign (\MdoVFourWallHits{} of
\MdoVFourTrials{} wall hits, \MdoVFourReflections{} reflections) within
\MdoSurvivalCalibrationGap; the per-cell wall-loss values are not used as the prior
because two cells hit the wall on every launch, and they are evaluated only as a
scenario below. The cusp probabilities enter solely through the declared closure
\texttt{\MdoClosureId} (\MdoClosureStatus), which multiplies the produced ionised
fraction by the survival $S$ and leaves the anode current unaffected; the
uncertain inputs are propagated through one frozen \MdoSampleCount-row Halton
sample (bases \MdoSampleBases; hash prefix \texttt{\MdoSampleSha}; frozen:
\MdoSampleFrozen). The \MdoObjectiveCount{} objectives are the model's ledgered
axial thrust and specific impulse (maximised), its thruster-electrical-to-beam
efficiency (maximised) and its anode input power (minimised); the constraint
requires the anode current to exceed the largest beam current over the sample
(\texttt{\MdoConstraintName} $\ge \MdoConstraintThreshold$~A), and a violating
design records no robust objectives. Each objective is made robust with the
\MdoRiskMeasure{} of the worst \MdoTailCount{} of \MdoSampleCount{} sample values
(tail fraction \MdoTailFraction). \MdoStrategyCount{} optimisers receive the same
\MdoInitialDesign-point initial design per seed and the same budget of
\MdoEvaluationsPerRun{} model evaluations: constrained qLogNEHVI from
\MdoBoLibrary{} \MdoBotorchVersion{} (independent Gaussian processes per output,
\MdoBoBatch{} candidates per iteration for \MdoBoIterations{} iterations,
\MdoBoMcSamples{} Monte Carlo samples, device \texttt{\MdoBoDevice} in
\texttt{\MdoBoDtype}), NSGA-III from \MdoNsgaLibrary{} \MdoPymooVersion{}
(population \MdoNsgaPopulation, \MdoNsgaGenerations{} generations) and a two-stage
Latin-hypercube baseline, each with seeds \MdoSeeds. Hypervolume is computed exactly
in a dimensionless all-maximise frame against the declared reference point (anode
power \MdoRefAnodePowerW{}~W; thrust, specific impulse and efficiency at zero) with
comparison scales of \MdoScaleThrust{}~N, \MdoScaleIsp{}~s, unity and
\MdoScalePower{}~W. A \MdoDenseCount-design Halton reference (seed \MdoDenseSeed;
\MdoDenseFeasible{} feasible, \MdoDenseInfeasible{} infeasible) supplies the robust
and nominal reference fronts (\MdoDenseRobustFront{} and \MdoDenseNominalFront{}
designs); it is not an optimiser run and not part of the comparison.

The protocol was frozen at preregistration commit \texttt{\MdoPreregCommit} after a
disclosed non-evidentiary shakedown of the complete production path
(\MdoShakedownBudget{} evaluations per run, seeds \MdoShakedownSeeds; disjoint from
the evidentiary design: \MdoShakedownDisjoint; passed: \MdoShakedownPassed;
evidentiary: \MdoShakedownEvidentiary), and executed exactly \MdoAttemptCount{} time
under an immutable Git-common lock (immutable: \MdoLockImmutable). The results are
committed at \texttt{\MdoResultsCommit} (manifest hash prefix
\texttt{\MdoManifestSha}; \MdoVerifiedFiles{} files verified byte for byte,
\MdoToleratedEolFiles{} accepted through any end-of-line tolerance) and the results
dashboard, whose embedded extraction the evidence generator requires to agree with
the sealed artifacts, at \texttt{\MdoDashboardCommit}. Acceptance is decided by
\MdoGateCount{} binding gates: bit-exact replay of every evaluation, model-domain
integrity, monotone hypervolume curves, exact budgets, shared initial designs, the
frozen sample hash, Pareto replay and the code contract (hashed source prefix
\texttt{\MdoSourceSha}, protocol hash prefix \texttt{\MdoProtocolSha}; matches:
\MdoCodeContractMatches). The optimiser comparisons, the design-set invariance and
the robust-versus-nominal differences were predeclared as reported outcomes that
never change the terminal state. The campaign ran on \MdoCpuCount{} CPU cores; a
CUDA device (\MdoCudaDevice) was probed and not used, and all timings are
diagnostic only.

\paragraph{Results.}
\EvidenceClaim{CLM-030}{The single execution terminated in state
\texttt{\MdoTerminalState} with \MdoGatesPassed{} of \MdoGateCount{} binding gates
passed, \MdoRuns{} runs of \MdoEvaluationsPerRun{} evaluations each
(\MdoTotalEvaluations{} model evaluations, \MdoInfeasibleEvaluations{} of them
constraint-violating, \MdoFailedRuns{} failed runs) and every evaluation replayed
bit-exactly (\MdoReplayed{} replayed, \MdoReplayMismatches{} mismatches). Within the
model classified as \texttt{\MdoClassification}, the final robust hypervolume of
constrained qLogNEHVI was \MdoHvBoA, \MdoHvBoB{} and \MdoHvBoC{} for seeds
\MdoSeeds{} (mean \MdoHvBoMean, sample standard deviation \MdoHvBoStd), against
\MdoHvNsgaA, \MdoHvNsgaB{} and \MdoHvNsgaC{} for NSGA-III (mean \MdoHvNsgaMean,
standard deviation \MdoHvNsgaStd) and \MdoHvLhsA, \MdoHvLhsB{} and \MdoHvLhsC{} for
the Latin-hypercube baseline (mean \MdoHvLhsMean, standard deviation
\MdoHvLhsStd). Paired by seed, qLogNEHVI exceeded the baseline in
\MdoBoBeatsRandomWins{} of \MdoBoBeatsRandomSeeds{} seeds and NSGA-III in
\MdoBoBeatsNsgaWins{} of \MdoBoBeatsNsgaSeeds{} seeds against a predeclared
requirement of \MdoBoBeatsRandomRequired{} (outcomes \MdoBoBeatsRandomPassed{} and
\MdoBoBeatsNsgaPassed); with \MdoEvaluationsPerRun{} evaluations it reached
\MdoAttainedBoMin{} to \MdoAttainedBoMax{} of the \MdoDenseCount-design
dense-reference robust hypervolume \MdoDenseRobustHv{} (mean
\MdoAttainedBoMeanTwo{} of the reference from a \MdoDenseToBudgetRatio-fold smaller
evaluation count), against \MdoAttainedNsgaMin{} to \MdoAttainedNsgaMax{} for
NSGA-III and \MdoAttainedLhsMin{} to \MdoAttainedLhsMax{} for the baseline. Final
Pareto-set sizes were \MdoParetoBoMin{} to \MdoParetoBoMax{} for qLogNEHVI,
\MdoParetoNsgaMin{} to \MdoParetoNsgaMax{} for NSGA-III and \MdoParetoLhsMin{} to

\MdoInfeasibleBoMax, \MdoInfeasibleNsgaMin{} to \MdoInfeasibleNsgaMax{} and

per run.}

\paragraph{Robust versus nominal fronts.}
\EvidenceClaim{CLM-032}{Pooled over the \MdoUniqueDesigns{} unique designs
evaluated by all runs, the robust front holds \MdoRobustFront{} designs
(hypervolume \MdoPooledRobustHv{} over \MdoRobustCandidates{} robust-feasible
designs) and the nominal front, a re-evaluation of the same designs at the prior
midpoints under the nominal constraint, holds \MdoNominalFront{} designs
(hypervolume \MdoPooledNominalHv{} over \MdoNominalCandidates{} nominally feasible
designs); the two fronts share \MdoSharedDesigns{} designs (Jaccard index
\MdoJaccard) and \MdoNominalRobustFeasible{} of the \MdoNominalFront{}
nominal-front designs are robust-feasible. The nominal front reaches an
electrical-to-beam efficiency of \MdoNominalEffMax{} and a specific impulse of
\MdoNominalIspMax{}~s, whereas the robust front reaches \MdoRobustEffMax{} and
\MdoRobustIspMax{}~s, both over anode powers from \MdoRobustPowerMin{} to

passed: \MdoSeparabilityPassed): under the declared closure the cusp probabilities

\paragraph{Sensitivity to the cusp prior.}
\EvidenceClaim{CLM-033}{Re-evaluating every recorded design under alternative
uniform cusp priors with upper bounds \MdoPriorUppers{} and the same frozen sample
rows, the robust nondominated design set restricted to the designs feasible under
both the alternative and the campaign prior was identical to the campaign set for
\MdoInvarianceIdenticalCount{} of \MdoPriorCount{} priors
(\MdoInvarianceExactCount{} in exact arithmetic; predeclared design-set invariance
\MdoInvariancePassed), while the feasible set itself moved from
\MdoPriorFeasibleMin{} to \MdoPriorFeasibleMax{} designs and the unrestricted
fronts from \MdoPriorFrontMin{} to \MdoPriorFrontMax{} designs (Jaccard index with
the campaign front as low as \MdoPriorJaccardMin). At fixed cusp probabilities the
\MdoRobustFront{} robust-Pareto designs respond as the closure dictates: the
scenario \texttt{\MdoScenarioJeffreysId}, which sets the per-cell probabilities to
the Jeffreys estimates of the wall-loss campaign's per-cell wall-hit counts
(\MdoJeffreysCellOne, \MdoJeffreysCellTwo, \MdoJeffreysCellThree{} and
\MdoJeffreysCellFour), gives a survival of \MdoScenarioJeffreysSurvival{} and a
maximum thrust of \MdoScenarioJeffreysThrustMax{}~N, that is no beam, so the
collisionless wall-hit probability of that campaign cannot be the per-cusp loss
probability of a sustained discharge in the cusp cascade; the scenario
\texttt{\MdoScenarioNoWallLossId} (survival \MdoScenarioNoWallLossSurvival, outside
the sampled support whose largest survival is \MdoSampledSurvivalMax) makes
\MdoScenarioNoWallLossInfeasible{} of the \MdoRobustFront{} robust-Pareto designs
violate the anode-current constraint, because the worst case over a finite sample
is the worst sampled case and not the worst case over the prior's support. The
frozen protocol also discloses that the corrected four-cell discharge solver closed
only for zero cusp probabilities in a probe of \MdoProbeTotalCases{} cases
(\MdoProbeClosedCases{} converged; residual floors from \MdoProbeResidualMin{} to
\MdoProbeResidualMax{} for every nonzero probability, about
\MdoProbeSecondsPerSolve{}~s per multistart solve), which is why the campaign
evaluates the declared closure and not that solver.}

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The boxed statement is the only interpretation offered here; it is bounded to the
model and closure and repeats the claim boundary sealed with the campaign.

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}
\textbf{Model-bounded interpretation.}
\EvidenceClaim{CLM-034}{This campaign is optimiser evidence about the
operating-point model under the declared closure \texttt{\MdoClosureId} and the
declared priors, classified \texttt{\MdoClassification}: it makes no
thruster-performance, plasma or physical-device claim, none of its fronts is a
design recommendation, calibration or validation, and every number is conditional
on that closure and on those priors. It supports no statement that any optimiser
is better beyond the recorded budget of \MdoEvaluationsPerRun{} evaluations, the
\MdoSeedCount{} seeds and this model. The \MdoExcludedVariableCount{} legacy
geometry variables are excluded because no geometry-to-model map survives the
audit, so the campaign says nothing about geometry. It is not a physics level: it
consumes the accepted operating-point model with a declared closure, opens none of
the field-resolved, coupled or experimental gates, and leaves the benchmark field
of the committed campaign policy null.}
\end{minipage}}
