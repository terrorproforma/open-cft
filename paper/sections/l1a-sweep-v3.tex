\subsection{Geometry sweep into the HEMP-like wall-radius-to-pitch regime}
\label{sec:l1a-sweep-v3-screening}

\paragraph{Design space and method.}
The campaign draws \SwtSobolDesigns{} designs by scrambled Sobol sampling (seed
\SwtSeed) over the \SwtVariableCount{} design variables of the accepted sweep of
Section~\ref{sec:l1a-sweep-v2}, keeping that sweep's builder rules, stage-count
mapping (\SwtStageMapping), alternating full-strength polarity, linear-vacuum
\SwtFieldModelLevel{} equivalent-current solver, domain
(\SwtGridRadial$\times$\SwtGridAxial{} intervals, radius \SwtDomainRadiusMm{}~mm),
solver tolerance (\SwtSolverRelTol) and the \SwtVTwoGateCount{} metric gates
verbatim, and widening \SwtWidenedVariableCount{} of the variables: the stage pitch
to \SwtBoxPitchLoMm{}--\SwtBoxPitchHiMm{}~mm (against
\SwtVTwoBoxPitchLoMm{}--\SwtVTwoBoxPitchHiMm), the wall radius to
\SwtBoxRadiusLoMm{}--\SwtBoxRadiusHiMm{}~mm (against
\SwtVTwoBoxRadiusLoMm{}--\SwtVTwoBoxRadiusHiMm), the magnet radial clearance to
\SwtBoxClearanceLoMm{}--\SwtBoxClearanceHiMm{}~mm and the magnet radial thickness to
\SwtBoxThicknessLoMm{}--\SwtBoxThicknessHiMm{}~mm, so that the wall-radius-to-pitch
ratio spans $r_w/L$ from \SwtBoxRwOverLLo{} to \SwtBoxRwOverLHi{}
($x_w = \pi r_w/L$ from \SwtBoxXwLo{} to \SwtBoxXwHi) and the sweep-\SwtPriorVersion{}
box is a strict subset (Table~\ref{tab:l1a-sweep-v3-box}). The widened bounds were
fixed from six corner solves against the metric gates before preregistration and
without reading any ratio. The \SwtHeldOutDesigns{} accepted designs of the earlier
sweep are rebuilt through the identity-proven pipeline of the geometry screening of
Section~\ref{sec:wall-loss-geometry-screening} and re-solved as a held-out set. Every
design is solved on the accepted map and on a \SwtRefinement$\times$ refined map;
the wall cusps and cells are found with the literature definition of
Section~\ref{sec:cusp-topology} imported unchanged from the frozen protocol of the
topology search (version \SwtTopologyVersion, commit
\texttt{\SwtTopologyResultsCommit}), and at every wall cusp the campaign reports the
HEMP design ratio of Koch et al.\ in four readings, of which the binding classifier is
the conservative one, $\rho = |B|(r_w, z_c)$ over the larger of the two adjacent axis
peaks; a design is HEMP-like when every one of its wall cusps has
$\rho \ge \SwtRhoThreshold$, the smaller of the two design values the THALES team
reported. The single-harmonic field theory reviewed in the bound literature review
(commit \texttt{\SwtLiteratureCommit}; sources \SwtLiteratureKeys) predicts
$\rho = \SwtIOne(x_w)$ for an infinite stack, hence the threshold
$\SwtXStarSymbol = \SwtXStar$ ($r_w/L = \SwtRwOverLStar$) at which
$\SwtIOne(\SwtXStarSymbol) = \SwtRhoThreshold$; the preregistered hypothesis
(\SwtHOne: $\rho$ tracks $\SwtIOne(x_w)$; \SwtHTwo: $\SwtXStarSymbol$ classifies the
designs) is reported at whatever value the campaign measures and is not a gate. The
protocol of experiment \texttt{\SwtExperimentId} was frozen at preregistration commit
\texttt{\SwtPreregCommit} after a disclosed non-evidentiary shakedown of the complete
production path on \SwtShakedownDesigns{} real designs of both sets (passed:
\SwtShakedownPassed; evidentiary: \SwtShakedownEvidentiary) at commit
\texttt{\SwtShakedownCommit}, and executed exactly \SwtAttemptCount{} time under an
immutable Git-common lock (immutable: \SwtLockImmutable) on \SwtWorkerPool{} of
\SwtCpuCount{} CPU cores (\texttt{\SwtDevice}; design stage \SwtStageWallMin{}~min,
assessment \SwtAssessmentWallMin{}~min). The results are committed at
\texttt{\SwtResultsCommit} (manifest hash prefix \texttt{\SwtManifestSha};
\SwtVerifiedFiles{} files verified byte for byte, \SwtToleratedEolFiles{} accepted
through any end-of-line tolerance; protocol \texttt{\SwtProtocolSha}, experiment code
\texttt{\SwtExperimentCodeSha}, dependency sources \texttt{\SwtDependencySourceSha}
over \SwtDependencySourceFiles{} files including the imported definition, field
pipeline \texttt{\SwtFieldPipelineSha} over \SwtFieldPipelineFiles{} files), together
with the results dashboard (commit \texttt{\SwtDashboardCommit}) whose embedded
extraction the evidence generator requires to agree with the sealed artifacts.
