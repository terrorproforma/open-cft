\subsection{Preregistered four-cell topology search: null result}
\label{sec:four-cell-v2}

\paragraph{Method.}
The search, classified \texttt{\FcnClassification}, asks whether the four-cell
wall-cusp topology assumed by the legacy design parameterisation exists as a
stable feature of the linear-vacuum \FcnFieldModelLevel{} field over the
original design variables. It draws
\FcnCandidateCount{} candidates (\FcnSamplingAlgorithm; \FcnDesignVariableCount{}
variables; \FcnStageCount{} strictly alternating magnet stages per candidate) and
solves \FcnMapCount{} independent maps per candidate: a primary map of
\FcnPrimaryRadial$\times$\FcnPrimaryAxial{} intervals, a downsampled map of
\FcnDownsampledRadial$\times$\FcnDownsampledAxial{} and an enlarged-domain map of
\FcnEnlargedRadial$\times$\FcnEnlargedAxial{} at \FcnEnlargedScale{} times the domain.
Under the frozen definition a candidate is stable only if every map contains
exactly \FcnRequiredCells{} interior cusps, where an interior cusp is a vector null
of the accepted $\psi$ map with finite-box boundary nulls excluded
(\FcnBoundaryNullPolicy), each cusp is registered to its geometry slot within
\FcnSlotErrorFraction{} of a pitch (\FcnCuspSlots), no cusp lies inside the
\FcnEndpointExclusionCells-cell endpoint exclusion, and the cusp positions shift
by at most \FcnMaxCuspShiftMm{} mm across the maps. Only a stable candidate would
have its constant-$\psi$ components traced at the flux quantiles
\FcnFluxQuantiles{} (saddle policy \texttt{\FcnSaddlePolicy}), its
\FcnElectronEnergyEv{} eV screening electrons checked for
$\rho_e/L_B \le \FcnGyroLimit$, and an exact $N=\FcnRequiredCells$ plasma network
coupled; the default publication is \FcnDefaultPublication, and the same-height
mirror proxy of the superseded first search is forbidden (allowed:
\FcnSameZProxyAllowed). The zero-pass policy was frozen before execution:
\FcnZeroPassPolicy. The protocol was executed once (single execution:
\FcnSingleExecution) at preregistration commit \texttt{\FcnPreregCommit}, results
commit \texttt{\FcnResultsCommit}, accepted coupling baseline
\texttt{\FcnCouplingCommit} and protocol payload \texttt{\FcnProtocolPayloadSha}. A
post-hoc audit (commit \texttt{\FcnAuditCommit}) found that \FcnToleratedEolFiles{}
bundle entry, the copied protocol whose recorded digest \texttt{\FcnProtocolRecordedSha}
was taken on a platform-line-ending checkout against \texttt{\FcnProtocolLfSha}
for the committed bytes, differs by end-of-line bytes only; the protocol payload
recomputes and every other bundle file is byte-exact. The evidence generator
tolerates exactly that file and nothing else.

\paragraph{Results.}
\EvidenceClaim{CLM-022}{Under the frozen cusp/cell definition, the four-cell
wall-cusp topology targeted by the original design parameterisation was not shown
stable for any candidate: \FcnStable{} of \FcnEvaluated{} candidates passed the
topology gates although all \FcnThreeMapAccepted{} passed every field gate on all
three maps. Every candidate recorded \texttt{TOPOLOGY\_COUNT}
(\FcnTopologyCountFailures{} of \FcnEvaluated) and \texttt{TOPOLOGY\_UNSTABLE}
(\FcnTopologyUnstableFailures{} of \FcnEvaluated) and no other failure code: the
primary maps hold \FcnPrimaryCuspMin{} to \FcnPrimaryCuspMax{} interior cusps per
candidate (\FcnPrimaryCuspTotal{} in total) against the required \FcnRequiredCells,
\FcnAnyExactCount{} candidates reached the exact count on any map, so no flux
surface was traced, no adiabaticity gate was evaluated (\FcnAdiabatic{} adiabatic),
no candidate was coupled (\FcnCoupled{} coupled), and \FcnUniqueStates{} plasma
states and \FcnPerformancePublications{} performance values were published. The
tolerance-based GPU replay reproduced the field components of all
\FcnGpuReplayRequired{} replay candidates to within \FcnGpuReplayMaxFieldDiff{} T and
\FcnGpuReplayMaxPsiDiff{} Wb, but the residual-diagnostic relative difference
exceeded its \FcnGpuReplayDiagnosticLimit{} limit for \FcnGpuReplayFailed{} of them
(\FcnGpuReplayFailedIds; maximum \FcnGpuReplayMaxDiagnostic), so the bundle records
\FcnGpuReplayPassed{} of \FcnGpuReplayRequired{} replay passes.}
Table~\ref{tab:four-cell-v2-failures} gives the complete failure taxonomy with its
counts and Table~\ref{tab:four-cell-v2-cusps} the interior-cusp counts per map.
The primary-map peak fields range from \FcnFieldPeakMin{} to \FcnFieldPeakMax{} T

which are diagnostics and never cells. The two top-ranked candidates
(\FcnRepresentativeIds) are archived with their geometry and all three field

\paragraph{Scope and lineage.}
\EvidenceClaim{CLM-024}{The null holds for the frozen definition of a stable
four-cell topology (exactly \FcnRequiredCells{} geometry-registered interior cusps on
all \FcnMapCount{} maps) over \FcnEvaluated{} candidates of one linear-vacuum
\FcnFieldModelLevel{} equivalent-current parameterisation; a null under this
definition is not proof that no such design exists, the screening fields are not
a permanent-magnet or nonlinear-iron material model, and no plasma state, mirror
probability or performance quantity is claimed. The superseded first search,
which screened \FcnLineageVOneCompatible{} of \FcnLineageVOneEvaluated{} candidates
as compatible through a deprecated same-height mirror proxy (status
\texttt{\FcnLineageVOneStatus}; preregistered: \FcnLineageVOnePreregistered; valid
for physical mirror claims: \FcnLineageVOneMirrorValid), and the two held-out
validations of the wall-cusp criterion, which failed with a
\FcnLineageWcvalOneException{} in \texttt{\FcnLineageWcvalOnePhase} after
\FcnLineageWcvalOneAttempted{} of \FcnLineageWcvalOneDeclared{} cases and with a

(criterion promoted: \FcnLineageWcvalOnePromoted, \FcnLineageWcvalTwoPromoted), are
