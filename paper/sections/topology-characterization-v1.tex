\subsection{Recorded topology characterization: no stable eligible cusp or cell}
\label{sec:topology-char-v1}

\paragraph{Method.}
The characterization is a preregistered developmental study, classified
\texttt{\TchClassification} (not a design optimization: \TchNotOptimization; not a
blind validation: \TchNotValidation), whose declared purpose reads
``\TchPurpose''. It covers a fixed family of \TchFamilyCaseCount{} cases: \TchStageLevels{} stage
counts (\TchStageCounts), pitches of \TchPitches{} mm, chamber radii of
\TchRadii{} mm and first polarities \TchPolarities, with the remaining geometry
frozen. Each case receives three independent solves of the \TchFieldModel{}
(primary \TchPrimaryRadial$\times$\TchPrimaryAxial{} intervals, refined
\TchRefinedRadial$\times$\TchRefinedAxial, enlarged domain
\TchEnlargedRadial$\times$\TchEnlargedAxial{} at \TchEnlargedScale{} times the
domain). Vector nulls are detected by \texttt{\TchRootDetector}, clustered at
\TchClusterFactor{} mesh cells and classified X, O or degenerate from the converged
Jacobian and winding index. Eligibility is fail-closed: an eligible cusp requires
that the \TchCuspEligibility; an eligible cell requires that the \TchCellEligibility;
and cell bounding requires an \TchCellBoundingRequirement. A stable root is one
\TchStableRootDefinition{} (\TchCorrespondenceAlgorithm). No mirror probability
(published: \TchMirrorPublished) or plasma state, power or performance quantity
(published: \TchPlasmaPublished) is computed; the allowed publication is
\TchPublicationAllowed. The protocol was executed once (single execution:
\TchSingleExecution) at preregistration commit \texttt{\TchPreregCommit}, results
commit \texttt{\TchResultsCommit}, accepted coupling baseline
\texttt{\TchCouplingCommit} and protocol identity \texttt{\TchProtocolSemanticSha};
every bundle file binds a canonical semantic identity and \TchToleratedEolFiles{}
end-of-line tolerances were needed.

\paragraph{Results.}

designs, all \TchThreeMapAccepted{} with three accepted maps and

preregistered eligibility rules found \TchStableEligibleCusps{} stable eligible
cusps and \TchStableEligibleCells{} stable eligible cells (\TchEligibleCuspsAnyMap{}
eligible cusps and \TchEligibleCellsAnyMap{} eligible cells on any single map).

detections; \TchXRoots{} X, \TchORoots{} O, \TchDegenerateRoots{} degenerate) were
