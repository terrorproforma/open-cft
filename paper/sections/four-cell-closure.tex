\paragraph{Derivation and numerical verification.}
The corrected ledger carries \FccRowCount{} residual rows in \FccStateDimension{}
unknowns and \FccPowerExpressionCount{} power expressions for \FccCellCount{} cells.
Its first \FccGlobalRowIndex{} rows (cathode emission, ionisation source currents,
electron continuity, cell energy balances, interface currents and cusp currents) are
mutually consistent and are parametrized exactly by the four cell potentials, which
the diagnostic \texttt{potential\_parametrized\_state} implements; the diagnostic
\texttt{global\_row\_closed\_form} evaluates the closed form displayed above. Both
diagnostics evaluate and never publish a state. The recomputation behind this
subsection draws \FccClosedFormSamples{} random admissible states (seed
\FccClosedFormSeed; anode voltages \FccSampleVoltages{}~V, anode currents

and the legacy lineage file, \FccBoundFileCount{} files in all, are bound at the
analysis commit \texttt{\FccAnalysisCommit} and verified unchanged at

\EvidenceClaim{CLM-037}{On the manifold of states that satisfy the first
\FccGlobalRowIndex{} rows of the corrected four-cell ledger, which the four cell
potentials parametrize exactly (largest normalized residual of those rows
\FccManifoldMaxResidual{} over the \FccClosedFormSamples{} seeded random states of the
recomputation, against a documented bound of \FccDocManifoldBound), the global power
row $R_{\FccGlobalRowIndex}$ reduces to the closed form displayed in
Section~\ref{sec:four-cell-closure}: \FccAnodeFallCoefficient{} times the transported
anode-cell electron current times the anode fall, plus the ionisation energy times
the electron current lost to the three interior cusps. The closed form agrees with the
evaluated row to a largest relative difference of \FccClosedFormRelDiff{} over those
\FccClosedFormSamples{} states (documented \FccDocClosedFormRelDiff{} over
\FccDocClosedFormSamples{} states; declared bound \FccClosedFormBound), and the
anode-fall coefficient recomputed at zero cusp probability is
\FccAnodeFallCoefficient{} against the ledger's \FccLedgerCoefficient. Inside the
admissible region, where the anode-cell potential is at or above the anode potential
and every current is non-negative, both terms are non-negative, so the corrected
equation set has no admissible root whenever any interior cusp probability is
positive.}

\paragraph{Attribution.}
\EvidenceClaim{CLM-040}{Both terms of the closed form are inherited from the source
formulation: assumption \FccKornfeldAssumption{} of \FccKornfeldId{} books ionisation
as a frozen loss and again as recombination heat at the boundaries, which is the
ionisation energy carried by the cusp-loss expression \texttt{\FccPcuspCurrent}, and
the printed anode electron term \texttt{\FccPanodeCurrent} carries the sign of an ion
under the restriction that the anode-cell potential is at or above the anode
potential, which is why the source's own published state and every rebuilt closure
sit on that boundary. The legacy \texttt{Power\_B\_EQs.m} (blob \texttt{\FccLegacyBlob})
carries the same terms on lines \FccLegacyCuspLine{} and \FccLegacyAnodeLine{}
(\FccLegacyIeTerms{} ionisation-energy terms on the cusp-loss line), and the rounded
published table of the source leaves a global-row misfit of \FccDocDmLedgerMisfit{} in
the ledger against \FccDmMisfit{} recomputed on the exact manifold at the published
potentials. The audit corrections of the rebuild (signed terminal ion row,
fourth-cell transport, excitation as a loss, cusp current subtracted in the ion rows)
cancel exactly in the substitution and do not appear in the closed form; they did not
introduce the inconsistency.}

\paragraph{Proposed correction (not accepted).}
\EvidenceClaim{CLM-041}{The minimal correction recorded in the ledger, dropping the
ionisation energy from the cusp-loss expression (\texttt{\FccPcuspProposed}) and giving
the anode electron term the electron sign (\texttt{\FccPanodeProposed}), has status
\texttt{\FccCorrectionStatus} and is not accepted here: with both changes the global
row is implied identically by the cell energy rows and charge conservation for every
cusp probability, the structural rank drops from \FccDocCorrectedRankBefore{} to
\FccDocCorrectedRankAfter{} (nullity \FccDocCorrectedNullity, all four potentials
free), and the model would need an independent potential closure, the
dielectric-cusp sheath relation that the source's cancelled cusp potentials were meant
to supply, before any state could be published; accepting the correction without that
closure would replace a non-closing model by an underdetermined one. A secondary
solver defect found alongside the analysis, an ordering projection that permuted
variable identities, was replaced by the isotonic projection and raised the zero-cusp
grid from \FccDocZeroCuspBefore{} to \FccDocZeroCuspAfter{} of \FccDocZeroCuspTotal{}
closures; it does not and cannot change the outcome for positive interior
probabilities.}
