\subsection{Material-aware confirmation of the HEMP-like designs under a linear-iron P2 field}
\label{sec:l1b-hemp-confirmation-screening}

\paragraph{Method.}
The campaign takes the \HmcDesignCount{} HEMP-like designs of the sweep of
Section~\ref{sec:l1a-sweep-v3}, the sealed catalogue's Sobol entries with
$\rho \ge \HmcRhoThreshold$ at every wall cusp (\HmcStageThreeDesigns,
\HmcStageFourDesigns{} and \HmcStageFiveDesigns{} designs of three, four and five stages;
\HmcCuspTwoDesigns, \HmcCuspThreeDesigns{} and \HmcCuspFourDesigns{} designs with two, three
and four wall cusps; $x_w$ from \HmcXwMin{} to \HmcXwMax, $r_w/L$ from \HmcRwOverLMin{} to
\HmcRwOverLMax; catalogue hash prefix \texttt{\HmcSweepCatalogueSha} at results revision
\texttt{\HmcSweepResultsCommit}), rebuilds each with the sweep's own builder with its identity
proven against the sealed design authorities and the sealed \HmcFieldModelLevelLOneA{} record
bytes, and solves its material-aware field with the adaptive quadratic finite-element
magnetostatic reference of the modern workstream: soft-iron pole pieces in the inter-magnet
gaps and a soft-iron return yoke at relative permeability \HmcIronMuR, recoil-remanence
SmCo-like magnets at \HmcMagnetRecoilMuR{} with axial, alternating remanence, and every other
region (\HmcVacuumLikeRegions) at unit permeability. The materials are linear and isotropic
(no saturation, no $B$--$H$ curve: \HmcLinearMaterials). The mesh is body-fitted and graded
(\HmcBoreElements{} elements across the bore radius, \HmcFeatureElements{} across the
thinnest feature, padding factor \HmcPaddingFactor, level-\HmcLevelZeroToken{}
minimum-angle gate \HmcAngleGateDeg$^\circ$); \HmcAdaptiveLevels{} nested levels are solved
(D\"orfler fraction \HmcDorflerTheta, red refinement, the level-\HmcLevelOneToken{} solution
prolonged from level \HmcLevelZeroToken) at a relative true residual of \HmcSolverRelTol{}
within \HmcSolverMaxIter{} iterations under a memory guard of \HmcRamBudgetFraction{} of the
free memory at start and a cap of \HmcDofCap{} degrees of freedom
(\texttt{\HmcSolverBackend}). The accepted map is the level-\HmcLevelOneToken{} solution
sampled on a regular grid of \HmcRadialIntervals{} radial intervals across the bore and
post-scaled by the design's sealed magnet-strength scale, so that both fields carry the same
magnet strength; the coarse map is the level-\HmcLevelZeroToken{} solution and the refined
map the same level-\HmcLevelOneToken{} solution at $\times$\HmcSamplingRefinement{} sampling.
Each map is characterised with the literature cusp definition of
Section~\ref{sec:cusp-topology}, imported unchanged from the frozen protocol of the topology
search (version \HmcTopologyVersion, commit \texttt{\HmcTopologyResultsCommit}; numerical
parameters equal: \HmcDefinitionParametersEqual), on the sealed \HmcFieldModelLevelLOneA{}
axis window, and the sweep's descriptors (the Koch ratio readings, the HEMP-like flag, the
wall harmonics) are evaluated on it. The comparison matches the wall cusps of the accepted P2
map to the sealed \HmcFieldModelLevelLOneA{} cusps by the definition's sorted
nearest-neighbour bijection within a per-design tolerance of one level-\HmcLevelZeroToken{}
bore element, never below the \HmcFieldModelLevelLOneA{} axial step
($\max(r_w/\HmcBoreElements, \Delta z)$ with $\Delta z = \HmcLOneADzMm$~mm;
\HmcToleranceMinMm{}--\HmcToleranceMaxMm~mm over the set); the tolerance is a discretisation
quantity fixed before any outcome was read. Two confirmation gates were predeclared: (b) the
fraction of designs whose wall-cusp count is unchanged (boundary-tolerant, with the strict
fraction reported) must reach \HmcGateBThreshold; (c) every matched cusp must shift by at
most its design tolerance and every design's matching must be a bijection (largest shift
over tolerance at most \HmcGateCThreshold); \HmcVerdictRule. The HEMP-like preservation, the
wall-field and Koch-ratio ratios, the axis nulls and the discretisation sensitivity are
reported and never gated. Binding for the whole bundle are \HmcBindingGateCount{} integrity
gates: every design resolved and identity-proven, every solve converged (gate (a)), every
axis null converged and every separatrix trace terminating cleanly, flux-consistent wall
traces, sampling stability at \HmcStabilityToleranceMm~mm, the axis window reproduced, the
memory policy, a bit-identical determinism replay and the hash bindings.

The protocol of experiment \texttt{\HmcExperimentId} was frozen at preregistration commit
\texttt{\HmcPreregCommit} after a disclosed non-evidentiary shakedown of the complete
production path on \HmcShakedownDesigns{} real designs of the set (passed:
\HmcShakedownPassed; evidentiary: \HmcShakedownEvidentiary) that also built and gated the
level-\HmcLevelZeroToken{} mesh of every one of the \HmcPreflightDesigns{} designs (passed:
\HmcPreflightPassed; smallest minimum angle \HmcPreflightMinAngleDeg$^\circ$), and executed
exactly \HmcAttemptCount{} time under an immutable Git-common lock (immutable:
\HmcLockImmutable; clean detached worktree attested: \HmcCleanWorktree) on \HmcWorkerPool{}
worker of \HmcCpuCount{} CPU cores, one design at a time, the GPU untouched
(\texttt{\HmcDevice}; design stage \HmcStageWallMin~min, assessment \HmcAssessmentWallMin~min,
lock to terminal \HmcExecutionWallMin~min; peak RSS \HmcPeakRssMb~MB, \HmcRamFractionUsed{} of
the budget). The results are committed at \texttt{\HmcResultsCommit} (manifest hash prefix
\texttt{\HmcManifestSha}; \HmcVerifiedFiles{} files verified byte for byte against the
manifest, \HmcToleratedEolFiles{} accepted through any end-of-line tolerance; protocol
\texttt{\HmcProtocolSha}, experiment code \texttt{\HmcExperimentCodeSha} over
\HmcExperimentCodeFiles{} files, dependency sources \texttt{\HmcDependencySourceSha} over
\HmcDependencySourceFiles{} files including the imported definition, field pipeline
\texttt{\HmcFieldPipelineSha} over \HmcFieldPipelineFiles{} files, each recomputed by the
