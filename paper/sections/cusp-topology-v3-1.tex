\subsection{Wall cusps and cells under the literature definition across the screened design sets}
\label{sec:cusp-topology-v3-1}

\paragraph{Definition and method.}
The campaign evaluates the HEMP/DCFT literature definition of a wall cusp on
prescribed vacuum field maps. A point cusp is an
axis null: a point on the symmetry axis where $B_z$ changes sign on the
axis-regular bicubic interpolant of the flux variable divided by the squared
radius, $g$, refined by bisection to a bracket of \CtvAxisBracketTolerance{}~m
(at most \CtvAxisMaxBisections{} bisections over \CtvAxisSamplesPerInterval{}
samples per grid interval). On the axis the analytic Jacobian of the field is
diagonal with eigenvalues of opposite sign, so every simple axis null is X-type;
the classification is checked against the frozen Jacobian and winding-index
classification of the characterization study, imported unchanged. The
separatrix of a null is the zero contour of $g$: it is traced from the null
along its radial eigen-direction (seed at \CtvSeedRadiusCellFraction{} of a
radial mesh cell) with an event-aware fourth-order field-line integrator
(arc-length step \CtvTraceStepCellFraction{} of the mesh cell, at most
\CtvWallEventMaxHalvings{} wall-event halvings, wall snap within
\CtvWallToleranceM{}~m) until it reaches the wall or leaves the map, and its
wall intersection $z_c$ must coincide with the root of
$\psi(r_w,z)-\psi_{\mathrm{axis}}$ within \CtvTraceFluxRootToleranceUm{}~$\mu$m
(binding; largest observed difference \CtvFluxRootMaxDiff{}~m over
\CtvWallTraceCount{} wall-reaching traces; the bilinear field-line step of the
wall-loss campaign's coupling records repeats every trace as a comparison and
differs by at most \CtvVFourBilinearMaxUm{}~$\mu$m). A wall cusp is an
intersection inside the straight dielectric; cells are the wall intervals
between consecutive cusps plus the anode-side and exit-side partial cells. The
descriptors of a cell are its length in stage pitches, its wall mirror ratio
(the weaker bounding cusp's wall field over the smallest wall field inside the
cell, \CtvWallSamplesPerCell{} samples) and its axis mirror ratio (that cusp
field over the largest axial field on the axis of the cell); both are field
ratios by construction. The definition follows \CtvLiteratureSourceCount{}
sources (\CtvLiteratureKeys) fixed in the reduced-model and topology literature
review bound at commit \texttt{\CtvLiteratureCommit}. Every
design is characterised on its accepted map and on a
\CtvRefinementFactor$\times$ refined map and is stable when the axis-null count
and the number of wall-reaching separatrices agree and no null or intersection
moves by more than \CtvStabilityToleranceMm{}~mm; a cusp within
\CtvBoundaryAmbiguityToleranceMm{}~mm of a straight-section end is flagged
boundary-ambiguous and reported.

\paragraph{Design sets and execution.}
The \CtvDesignCount{} designs come in \CtvSetCount{} sets: the
\CtvSweepCount{} accepted designs of the geometry sweep of
Section~\ref{sec:l1a-sweep-v2} (\CtvSweepStages{} magnet stages), re-solved on
the CPU with the identity-proven pipeline of the wall-loss geometry screening
(sealed manifest \texttt{\CtvSweepManifestSha}, preregistration
\texttt{\CtvSweepPreregCommit}); the \CtvFourCellCount{} sealed candidates of
the four-cell search of Section~\ref{sec:four-cell-v2} (dataset
\texttt{\CtvFourCellDatasetSha}), whose source policy gives the even stages
\CtvFourCellStrengthRatioMin{} to \CtvFourCellStrengthRatioMax{} of the odd
stages' strength; the \CtvCharVCount{} cases of the characterization of
Section~\ref{sec:topology-char-v1} (\CtvCharVStages{} stages; dataset
\texttt{\CtvCharVDatasetSha}), whose sealed axis roots are a held-out
reference; and the P2-qualified divergent-exit-stack field of the wall-loss
campaign of Section~\ref{sec:wall-loss-v4} (checkpoints
\texttt{\CtvPTwoPrimaryCheckpointSha} and \texttt{\CtvPTwoRefinedCheckpointSha}),
a finite-element field with iron poles and a return yoke whose straight
dielectric spans \CtvPTwoStraightStartMm{} to \CtvPTwoStraightEndMm{}~mm of a
\CtvPTwoChamberLengthMm{}~mm chamber with \CtvPTwoStages{} stages at a pitch of
\CtvPTwoPitchMm{}~mm; for that row the refined map is the level-two
finite-element checkpoint sampled on a twice-refined grid, not a re-solve. The
three linear-vacuum sets are \CtvFieldModelLevel{} screening fields and carry
the label \texttt{\CtvClassification}; the P2 row carries
\texttt{\CtvPTwoClassification}. The held-out references are binding: the
sealed axis nulls of the sweep and the sealed in-channel axis roots of the
characterization must each be in bijection with the campaign's channel nulls
within \CtvHeldOutToleranceUm{}~$\mu$m. The tracing grid keeps at least
\CtvSweepRadialCellsMin{} radial cells across a bore (\CtvPTwoRadialCells{} for
the P2 row).

The protocol of experiment \texttt{\CtvExperimentId} was frozen at
preregistration commit \texttt{\CtvPreregCommit} after a disclosed
non-evidentiary shakedown of the complete production path on
\CtvShakedownDesigns{} real designs of every set (passed: \CtvShakedownPassed;
evidentiary: \CtvShakedownEvidentiary), among them the case on which the
predecessor's held-out extraction had failed
(\texttt{\CtvShakedownDefectDesign}), and executed exactly \CtvAttemptCount{}

\CtvStageWallMin{}~min, assessment \CtvAssessmentWallMin{}~min). The
\CtvBindingGateCount{} binding integrity gates require every declared design
resolved, every identity proven, every axis null converged, every separatrix
trace terminating cleanly, every wall trace consistent with its flux root,
refinement stability, the held-out correspondences, bit-identical determinism
replays and the hash bindings to the frozen authorities; the cusp-count
distribution, the legacy-target fractions, the mirror-ratio ranges and the P2
consistency differences are reported and not gated. The results are committed
at \texttt{\CtvResultsCommit} (manifest hash prefix

over \CtvFieldPipelineFiles{} files), together with the results dashboard
