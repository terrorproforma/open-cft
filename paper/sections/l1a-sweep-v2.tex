\subsection{Accepted geometry sweep: field-only design-space screening}
\label{sec:l1a-sweep-v2}

\paragraph{Method.}
The sweep evaluates \SwpEvaluated{} designs drawn by the \SwpSamplingAlgorithm{}
(seed \SwpSeed) over \SwpDesignVariableCount{} design variables of the geometry
model, including stage pitch, chamber radius, dielectric thickness, magnet
dimensions, source strength, exit length and expansion, first polarity and a
stage-count selector mapped as \texttt{\SwpStageMapping} (\SwpStageMin{} to
\SwpStageMax{} stages). Each design is solved once with the linear-vacuum
\SwpModelLevel{} equivalent-current axisymmetric solver on a
\SwpGridRadial$\times$\SwpGridAxial{} grid (domain radius \SwpDomainRadiusMm{} mm,
$z$ from \SwpDomainZMinMm{} to \SwpDomainZMaxMm{} mm) to a relative residual of
\SwpSolverRelTol; the magnet source is a \SwpPreviewDescription{} (authoritative:
\SwpPreviewAuthoritative). An axis cusp is a sign change of the on-axis $B_z$
between adjacent stages, and the per-cell mirror ratio is defined by the frozen
QoI policy as the \SwpMirrorRatioDefinition; the policy itself states that the
topology quantities are a \SwpTopologyClaim. Acceptance requires zero case
failures (\SwpZeroFailuresRequired) and \SwpGateCount{} terminal gates; \SwpParityCaseCount{}
designs are re-solved on the CPU for backend parity; \SwpObjectiveCount{}
preregistered objectives define the non-dominated set and \SwpRoleCount{} roles
select representatives whose full field artifacts are archived. The protocol was
executed exactly \SwpMaxExecutions{} time (preregistration commit
\texttt{\SwpPreregCommit}, results commit \texttt{\SwpResultsCommit}, protocol
payload \texttt{\SwpProtocolPayloadSha}; \SwpTimingPolicy). A post-hoc audit
(commit \texttt{\SwpAuditCommit}) found that \SwpToleratedEolFiles{} frozen
sidecar, the protocol digest \texttt{\SwpProtocolRecordedSha} recorded on a
platform-line-ending checkout against \texttt{\SwpProtocolLfSha} for the
committed bytes, differs by end-of-line bytes only; the protocol payload
recomputes and every file of the results bundle is byte-exact. The evidence
generator tolerates exactly that file and nothing else.

\paragraph{Scope.}
\EvidenceClaim{CLM-021}{The sweep is field-only screening classified
\texttt{\SwpClassification}: its fields are linear-vacuum \SwpModelLevel{}
equivalent-current models, no permanent-magnet or nonlinear-iron material model
enters the screening, and the only finite-element field admitted elsewhere in
this manuscript is the P2 reference of the wall-loss campaign; the axis cusps
are sampled-axis descriptors, not a continuous critical-point proof; the sweep
computes no thrust, efficiency, plasma, thermal or structural quantity, carries
no hardware validation and opens no physics level.}
