\subsection{Collisionless test-particle wall loss across the accepted sweep designs}
\label{sec:wall-loss-geometry-screening-v1}

\paragraph{Method.}
The screening integrates collisionless prescribed-field test-particle electron
orbits (orbit\_mc \WlgOrbitMcVersion, \texttt{\WlgBackend}; contract matches:
\WlgOrbitMcContractMatches) in the fields of every accepted design of the geometry
sweep of Section~\ref{sec:l1a-sweep-v2}: the \WlgPrimaryCount{} non-dominated designs
as the primary batch and the remaining \WlgExtensionCount{} accepted designs as an
extension batch whose inclusion the frozen protocol tied to the shakedown's timing
projection (projected \WlgTimingProjectedMin{}~min against a budget of
\WlgTimingBudgetMin{}~min; within budget: \WlgTimingWithinBudget). Only the sweep's
\WlgRepresentativeCount{} representatives carry stored full-field maps, so every
design is rebuilt with the sweep's own case builder and re-solved with the accepted
linear-vacuum \WlgFieldModelLevel{} solver (\texttt{\WlgResolveSolver}) at the sweep
resolution (\WlgGridRadial$\times$\WlgGridAxial{} intervals, relative residual
\WlgSolverRelTol) before any orbit is integrated. Identity is proven per design:
the rebuilt geometry, source, configuration and case hashes must equal the sealed
sweep record (manifest prefix \texttt{\WlgSweepManifestSha}, raw results
\texttt{\WlgSweepRawSha}), the re-solved quantities of interest must reproduce the
recorded ones within the sweep's replay tolerances, and the representatives must
reproduce their stored maps node-wise within \WlgStoredPsiTol{}~Wb and
\WlgStoredBTol{}~T (largest observed differences \WlgStoredPsiMaxDiff{}~Wb and
\WlgStoredBMaxDiff{}~T); \WlgIdentityProvenDesigns{} of \WlgDesignCount{} designs
passed. The bore interpolant must reproduce the solver field within an rms of
\WlgInterpolationGate{} (largest observed \WlgInterpolationRmsMax); a
\WlgRefinement$\times$ refined re-solve is a field-resolution diagnostic
(cross-resolution gate \WlgCrossResolutionGate, largest observed
\WlgCrossResolutionRmsMax) that the bundle records for the
\WlgCrossResolutionDesigns{} representatives only, which additionally run a
refined-field orbit case. The fields keep the sweep's status
\texttt{\WlgFieldStatus}: they are screening fields and not the P2 reference of
Section~\ref{sec:wall-loss-v4}.

Each design receives \WlgLaunchesPerCase{} launches in \WlgStrataPerCase{} equal-weight
strata: \WlgCellCount{} axial cells at fractions \WlgCellFractions{} of the design's
straight span between the injector zone and the end of the straight dielectric, two
radii at \WlgRadiusFractions{} of the wall radius, kinetic energies of
\WlgEnergies{}~eV, pitch angles of \WlgPitches$^\circ$, parallel directions
\WlgDirections{} and \WlgGyrophaseCount{} gyrophases (offset rule
\WlgGyrophaseOffsetRule), with the \texttt{\WlgEstimator} estimator and
\texttt{\WlgIntervalMethod} intervals. As in the prior campaign, wall-hit authority
is the straight cylindrical dielectric; an electron that reaches the wall radius
beyond the end of the straight section, the anode plane or the exit plane is a
domain escape whose sub-class is recorded post hoc. Each orbit may travel
\WlgMaxPathLengths{} channel lengths within \WlgMaxTimeFactor{} times the transit
time of a \WlgSlowestEnergy{}~eV electron over that path (event tolerance
\WlgEventTolerance{}~m, step budget \WlgMaxSteps, Lorentz-factor guard
\WlgMaxGamma). Every design runs two timestep policies (maximum gyro-rotation per
step \WlgRotationN{} and \WlgRotationTwoN{}~rad); the finer case is reported and the
pair defines the per-design convergence flag (\WlgReportedCase). Binding for the
whole dataset are the field-adapter gates, the campaign preflight, zero numerical
failures, the exact energy gate (\WlgEnergyGate), equality of final and event
velocity, the wall-endpoint gate (\WlgWallEndpointGate{}~m), earliest-event
ordering, the runtime rotation bound, exact deterministic replay of every case and
a cross-process determinism sample (\WlgPerDesignChecks{} per-design checks and
\WlgManufacturedChecks{} manufactured checks: numpy versus Warp CPU relative
velocity difference \WlgCpuParityDiff; CUDA parity \texttt{\WlgCudaParityStatus});
the per-design flag requires a change under timestep halving of at most
\WlgMaxChangeGate{} with overlapping intervals and seals the orbit artifacts only
when it holds. Timeouts
are reported, not gated, and the magnetic-moment variation is
\texttt{\WlgMuRole} (per-design medians from \WlgMuMedianMin{} to \WlgMuMedianMax,
largest single value \WlgMuMaxMax).

The protocol was frozen at preregistration commit \texttt{\WlgPreregCommit} after a
disclosed non-evidentiary shakedown on \WlgShakedownDesigns{} real re-solved designs
at \WlgShakedownLaunches{} launches each (\WlgShakedownValidators{} validator calls;
disjoint from the evidentiary design: \WlgShakedownDisjoint; passed:
\WlgShakedownPassed; evidentiary: \WlgShakedownEvidentiary), and executed exactly
\WlgAttemptCount{} time under an immutable Git-common lock (immutable:
\WlgLockImmutable) on \WlgWorkerPool{} of \WlgCpuCount{} CPU cores
(\texttt{\WlgDevice}; case pool \WlgCasesWallMin{}~min, assessment
\WlgAssessmentWallMin{}~min). The results are committed at
\texttt{\WlgResultsCommit} (manifest hash prefix \texttt{\WlgManifestSha};
\WlgVerifiedFiles{} files verified byte for byte, \WlgToleratedEolFiles{} accepted
through any end-of-line tolerance; orbit\_mc source \texttt{\WlgOrbitMcSourceSha},
field pipeline \texttt{\WlgFieldPipelineSha} over \WlgFieldPipelineFiles{} files,
protocol \texttt{\WlgProtocolSha}, experiment code \texttt{\WlgExperimentCodeSha}),
together with the results dashboard whose embedded extraction the evidence
generator requires to agree with the sealed artifacts. The frozen protocol
discloses the prior campaign as ``\WlgVFourHeadline''.
