\subsection{Catalogue optimisation over the screened sweep designs under the declared test-particle wall-loss closure}
\label{sec:mdo-l0-v2}

\paragraph{Method.}
The design space is the product of a categorical catalogue index and the operating
point of the prior campaign. The catalogue is the set of \MdbCatalogueSize{} accepted
sweep designs screened in Section~\ref{sec:wall-loss-geometry-screening}
(classification \texttt{\MdbScreeningClassification}), each carried with its sealed
geometry and its wall-hit counts at the \texttt{\MdbReportedCase} timestep
(\MdbCellTrials{} launches in each of \MdbCellCount{} launch cells, \MdbPooledTrials{}
pooled); the screening dataset is bound by bytes, by Git blob at the screening record
commit \texttt{\MdbScreeningResultsCommit} (blob prefix
\texttt{\MdbScreeningDatasetBlob}), by its manifest entry and by ancestry, and the
\MdbCatalogueBindingChecks{} binding checks are a binding gate (passed:
\MdbCatalogueBindingPassed; sealed catalogue identity prefix
\texttt{\MdbCatalogueSha}). The optimisers choose a member of the catalogue, never a
geometry value, and no surrogate stands between the counts and the model: the
\MdbRejectedSurrogates{} surrogates fitted to the same dataset were rejected on their
predeclared gates (\texttt{\MdbSurrogateOneOutcome} at \texttt{\MdbSurrogateOneCommit},
\texttt{\MdbSurrogateTwoOutcome} at \texttt{\MdbSurrogateTwoCommit}). The
\MdbOperatingVariableCount{} operating-point variables keep the prior campaign's
domain: the discharge voltage from \MdbUaLower{} to \MdbUaUpper{} V, the anode current
from \MdbIaLower{} to \MdbIaUpper{} A and the propellant mass flow from \MdbMdotLower{}
to \MdbMdotUpper{} kg/s. Each design carries \MdbUncertainInputCount{} uncertain inputs:
its \MdbCellCount{} per-cell wall-hit probabilities as Jeffreys Beta posteriors of its
own counts (pseudo-count \MdbJeffreysPrior; no rounded probability is frozen anywhere,
every posterior mean being recomputed from the counts by the evidence generator), the
produced ionised fraction on $[\MdbEtaLower, \MdbEtaUpper]$, the doubly charged ion
share on $[\MdbZetaLower, \MdbZetaUpper]$ and the divergence factor on
$[\MdbGammaLower, \MdbGammaUpper]$, propagated through the prior campaign's identical
\MdbSampleCount{} Halton unit rows (bases \MdbSampleBases; unit-row hash prefix
\texttt{\MdbUnitRowsSha}, per-design sample hash prefix
\texttt{\MdbCatalogueSampleSha}; frozen: \MdbSampleFrozen) pushed through each
design's posterior quantile functions. The probabilities enter solely through the
declared campaign closure \texttt{\MdbClosureId} (\MdbClosureShort;
\MdbClosureStatus), the multiplicative survival of the prior campaign with the
screening probability of each launch cell in place of a prior: it identifies a
collisionless test-particle wall-hit probability on a linear-vacuum screening field
with a per-cusp survival factor, an identification the prior campaign's scenario
analysis showed is not the per-cusp loss probability of a sustained discharge
(Section~\ref{sec:mdo-l0}), so every number here is conditional on it. A declared
sensitivity closure \texttt{\MdbSensitivityClosureId} (\MdbSensitivityClosureShort;
\MdbSensitivityClosureStatus) replaces the cascade by the pooled probability over all
\MdbPooledTrials{} launches and is evaluated on the recorded designs only. The
corrected four-cell discharge solver is not used because it has no admissible root for
interior cusp probabilities (Section~\ref{sec:four-cell-closure}; analysis commit
\texttt{\MdbFourCellClosureCommit}). The \MdbObjectiveCount{} objectives, their
comparison scales (\MdbScaleThrust{}~N, \MdbScaleIsp{}~s, unity, \MdbScalePower{}~W),
the reference point (anode power \MdbRefAnodePowerW{}~W; thrust, specific impulse and
efficiency at zero), the \MdbRiskMeasure{} of the worst \MdbTailCount{} of
\MdbSampleCount{} sample values (tail fraction \MdbTailFraction), the worst-sampled-case
constraint (\texttt{\MdbConstraintName} $\ge \MdbConstraintThreshold$~A) and the fixed
cathode power (\MdbCathodePowerW{}~W) and PPU efficiency (\MdbPpuEfficiency) are the
prior campaign's (same frame: \MdbSameReferenceFrame), so hypervolumes are comparable
between the two campaigns.

\MdbStrategyCount{} optimisers receive the same \MdbInitialDesign-point initial design
per seed (\MdbInitialDesign{} distinct catalogue designs; identical across optimisers:
\MdbSharedInitialIdentical) and the same budget of \MdbEvaluationsPerRun{} design
evaluations: constrained qLogNEHVI from \MdbBoLibrary{} \MdbBotorchVersion{} over a
list of mixed Gaussian processes with a categorical kernel on the catalogue index and
a Mat\'ern kernel with automatic relevance determination on the operating point
(\MdbBoBatch{} candidates per iteration for \MdbBoIterations{} iterations,
\MdbBoMcSamples{} Monte Carlo samples, device \texttt{\MdbBoDevice} in
\texttt{\MdbBoDtype} with \MdbBoThreads{} threads), whose acquisition is maximised in
two stages, an exhaustive discrete stage over every catalogue design with
\MdbBoCandidatesPerDesign{} Latin-hypercube operating points each
(\MdbBoCandidateCount{} candidates) followed by a gradient refinement of each chosen
member's operating point at its fixed catalogue index (at most \MdbBoRefineMaxiter{}
L-BFGS-B iterations; \MdbBoRefinementsAccepted{} of \MdbBoRefinements{} refinements
accepted); NSGA-III from \MdbNsgaLibrary{} \MdbPymooVersion{} with mixed variables,
duplicate elimination in the population and in mating (population \MdbNsgaPopulation,
\MdbNsgaGenerations{} generations; \MdbNsgaReferenceDirectionCount{} energy reference
directions from seed \MdbNsgaReferenceDirectionSeed);
and a two-stage Latin-hypercube baseline over the catalogue and the operating point
(\MdbInitialDesign{} shared points then \MdbSecondStage{} further points), each with
seeds \MdbSeeds. Hypervolume is computed exactly in the dimensionless all-maximise
frame. A dense reference of every catalogue design crossed with one
\MdbDensePointsPerDesign-point operating-point grid (\MdbDenseCount{} evaluations, seed
\MdbDenseSeed, \MdbDenseWorkers{} worker processes, \MdbDenseEvaluationSeconds{}~s;
\MdbDenseFeasible{} feasible, \MdbDenseInfeasible{} infeasible) supplies the robust and
nominal reference fronts (\MdbDenseRobustFront{} and \MdbDenseNominalFront{} points on
catalogue designs \MdbDenseRobustFrontDesigns{} and \MdbDenseNominalFrontDesigns) and,
per design, the design's own robust hypervolume; it is not an optimiser run and not
part of the comparison. The pooled designs are also re-evaluated under the sensitivity
closure and under per-cell posteriors whose widths are rescaled by
\MdbWidthAlternativeCount{} alternative factors while their means are kept.

The protocol was frozen at preregistration commit \texttt{\MdbPreregCommit} after a
disclosed non-evidentiary shakedown of the complete production path
(\MdbShakedownBudget{} evaluations per run, seeds \MdbShakedownSeeds,
\MdbShakedownDensePoints{} dense points per design; disjoint from the evidentiary
design: \MdbShakedownDisjoint; passed: \MdbShakedownPassed; evidentiary:
\MdbShakedownEvidentiary; import scope equal to the hash scope:
\MdbShakedownImportScopeMatches), and executed exactly \MdbAttemptCount{} time under an
immutable Git-common lock (immutable: \MdbLockImmutable). The results are committed at
\texttt{\MdbResultsCommit} (manifest hash prefix \texttt{\MdbManifestSha};
\MdbVerifiedFiles{} files verified byte for byte, \MdbToleratedEolFiles{} accepted
through any end-of-line tolerance) and the results dashboard, whose embedded extraction
of both campaigns the evidence generator requires to agree with the sealed artifacts,
at \texttt{\MdbDashboardCommit}. Acceptance is decided by \MdbGateCount{} binding
gates, every one of them a recording-integrity gate: bit-exact replay of every
evaluation, model-domain integrity, monotone hypervolume curves up to a roundoff
tolerance of \MdbHvMonotoneTolerance{} (largest recorded decrease
\MdbHvMonotoneLargestDecrease), exact budgets, shared initial designs, the frozen
sample hashes, Pareto replay, the code contract (hashed source prefix
\texttt{\MdbSourceSha}, protocol hash prefix \texttt{\MdbProtocolSha}; matches:
\MdbCodeContractMatches), the catalogue binding, the equality of the hash scope with
the files actually imported, duplicate elimination in NSGA-III and the consistency of
every descriptive label. The protocol declares that acceptance therefore means
recorded consistently and not that any optimiser is effective; the optimiser
comparisons, the robust-versus-nominal, closure and width differences and the
catalogue designs on the fronts are reported outcomes that never change the terminal
state, and every efficacy statement carries its seed count. The campaign ran on
\MdbCpuCount{} CPU cores; a CUDA device (\MdbCudaDevice) was probed and not used, and
all timings are diagnostic only.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}

\EvidenceClaim{CLM-059}{This campaign is optimiser evidence about the operating-point
model over a discrete catalogue of \MdbCatalogueSize{} screened designs under the
declared closure \texttt{\MdbClosureId} (\MdbClosureStatus), classified
\texttt{\MdbClassification}: it makes no thruster-performance, plasma or
physical-device claim, none of its fronts is a design recommendation, calibration or
validation, and every number is conditional on the declared identification of a
collisionless test-particle wall-hit probability on a linear-vacuum screening field
(classification \texttt{\MdbScreeningClassification}) with a per-cusp survival factor,
an identification that the prior campaign's scenario analysis showed is not the
per-cusp loss probability of a sustained discharge; a design that wins here wins under
this closure only. No surrogate stands between the screening counts and the model
(\MdbRejectedSurrogates{} surrogates over the same dataset were rejected on their
predeclared gates: \texttt{\MdbSurrogateOneOutcome}, \texttt{\MdbSurrogateTwoOutcome}),
so geometry enters only through the \MdbCatalogueSize{} sealed designs and nothing is
interpolated between them. It supports no statement that any optimiser is better
beyond the recorded budget of \MdbEvaluationsPerRun{} evaluations, the \MdbSeedCount{}
seeds and this model. It is not a physics level: it solves no field, maps no geometry
to the model beyond the declared closure, opens none of the field-resolved, coupled or
experimental gates, and leaves the benchmark field of the committed campaign policy
null.}
