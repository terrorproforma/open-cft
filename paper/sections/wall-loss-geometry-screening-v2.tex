\subsection{Collisionless wall access from the catalogue cells of the accepted sweep designs}
\label{sec:wall-loss-geometry-screening-v2-screening}

\paragraph{Method.}
The screening integrates collisionless prescribed-field test-particle electron
orbits (orbit\_mc \WlhOrbitMcVersion, \texttt{\WlhBackend}; contract matches:
\WlhOrbitMcContractMatches) in the re-solved fields of every accepted design of the
geometry sweep of Section~\ref{sec:l1a-sweep-v2}, launched from the separatrix-bounded
wall cells of the catalogue admitted in Section~\ref{sec:cusp-topology}
(catalogue hash prefix \texttt{\WlhCatalogueSha}, \WlhCatalogueDesigns{} catalogue
designs at results revision \texttt{\WlhCatalogueResultCommit}; the
\WlhSweepDesignCount{} sweep designs carry \WlhSweepCellCount{} cells, \WlhAnodeCells{}
anode-side partial cells between the anode plane and the first wall cusp,
\WlhInteriorCells{} interior cells between consecutive wall cusps and \WlhExitCells{}
exit-side partial cells between the last wall cusp and the end of the straight
dielectric). The cells are bound by the catalogue bytes and their descriptors are
carried as cell geometry only; the catalogue's mirror ratios are field ratios, never
probabilities. Every cell launches at its midpoint (\texttt{\WlhLaunchPlaneRule}): a
midpoint inside the injector zone is flagged and never moved
(\WlhInjectorFlaggedCells{} such cell, design \texttt{\WlhInjectorFlaggedDesign},
\WlhInjectorFlaggedLengthMm~mm long), and cells shorter than
\WlhShortCellLengthMm~mm are flagged (\WlhShortCells{} cells). Each cell receives
\WlhStrataPerCell{} equal-weight strata of kinetic energy \WlhEnergies~eV, pitch
angle \WlhPitches$^\circ$ and parallel direction \WlhDirections; within a stratum the
launch radius band (\WlhBandOneLo--\WlhBandOneHi{} or \WlhBandTwoLo--\WlhBandTwoHi{} of
the wall radius), the radius inside the band and the gyrophase are drawn from an
independent scrambled Sobol' sequence per design, cell and stratum
(\WlhSobolGenerator), so no two designs share a launch set. The frozen two-stage
allocation rule gives every cell \WlhStageOnePerCell{} launches
(\WlhStageOnePointsPerStratum{} points per stratum) and tops a cell up to
\WlhFinalPerTopped{} (\WlhStageTwoBlocks{} further blocks, \WlhStageTwoPointsPerStratum{}
points per stratum) only when its stage-one Wilson interval for the wall-access
fraction is wider than \WlhWidthThreshold; the rule is evaluated by code inside the
single execution and replayed by the main process from the endpoint terminations. A
frozen-seed subset of \WlhControlFraction{} of every cell's final launches
(\WlhControlOfStageOne{} or \WlhControlOfTopped{} launches) is re-integrated at the
halved time step (maximum gyro-rotation per step \WlhRotationN{} and
\WlhRotationTwoN~rad; policies \texttt{\WlhTimestepN} and \texttt{\WlhTimestepControl}) and
paired orbit by orbit with its \texttt{\WlhTimestepN}-step partner; the pooled paired
difference is the campaign-binding control gate (\WlhControlGateMax) and the
per-design paired flag decides sealing. Per cell the stage counts are pooled with
equal weights into \texttt{\WlhEstimator} estimates with \texttt{\WlhIntervalMethod}
intervals for the wall, reflection, escape and timeout fractions, with the binomial
floor and the Jeffreys floor recorded; a cell is surrogate-ready when its Jeffreys
floor is at most \WlhReadinessFloor. The estimand of every cell is a collisionless
geometric wall-access fraction of the launch distribution (\WlhEstimandStatement);
design values are wall-area-weighted and launch-weighted averages of the per-cell
fractions and are declared averages, not estimands in their own right.

Every sweep design is rebuilt with the sweep's own case builder and re-solved with the
accepted linear-vacuum \WlhFieldModelLevel{} solver (\texttt{\WlhResolveSolver},
\WlhGridRadial$\times$\WlhGridAxial{} intervals) with its identity proven against the
sealed sweep record (manifest prefix \texttt{\WlhSweepManifestSha}, raw results
\texttt{\WlhSweepRawSha}); the \WlhStoredMapDesigns{} representatives reproduce their
stored maps node-wise within \WlhStoredPsiMaxDiff~Wb and \WlhStoredBMaxDiff~T. Unlike
the fixed-fraction screening of Section~\ref{sec:wall-loss-geometry-screening}, the
\WlhRefinement$\times$ refined re-solve and the cross-resolution diagnostic run for every
design: \WlhCrossResolutionDesigns{} of \WlhDesignCount{} designs, largest cross-resolution
rms error \WlhCrossResolutionRmsMax{} against a gate of \WlhCrossResolutionGate{} and
largest interpolation rms error \WlhInterpolationRmsMax{} against \WlhInterpolationGate;
\WlhIdentityProvenDesigns{} of \WlhDesignCount{} designs passed every field check. The
sweep fields keep the status \texttt{\WlhFieldStatus}. One further row integrates the
divergent-exit design of Section~\ref{sec:wall-loss-v4} on its qualified field through the
hash-bound adapter of that campaign (\WlhPTwoFieldLevel) with this campaign's launch
design in its \WlhPTwoCellCount{} catalogue cells; it carries the label
\texttt{\WlhPTwoLabel} and is a launch-design row, not a replication of that campaign's
accepted evidence (not a replication: \WlhPTwoNotReplication). Wall-hit authority is the
straight cylindrical dielectric as in that campaign; each orbit may travel
\WlhMaxPathLengths{} channel lengths within \WlhMaxTimeFactor{} times the transit time of
a \WlhSlowestEnergy~eV electron over that path (event tolerance \WlhEventTolerance~m,
step budget \WlhMaxSteps, Lorentz-factor guard \WlhMaxGamma; largest step count of any
case \WlhStepsMax). Binding for the whole dataset are the field-adapter gates, the
campaign preflight, zero numerical failures, the exact energy gate (\WlhEnergyGate),
equality of final and event velocity, the wall-endpoint gate (\WlhWallEndpointGate~m),
earliest-event ordering, the runtime rotation bound, the catalogue binding, the frozen
stage-one authority, Wilson-exact case sizes, the allocation-rule replay, exact
deterministic replay of every case and a cross-process determinism sample
(\WlhPerDesignChecks{} per-design checks and \WlhManufacturedChecks{} manufactured
checks: numpy versus Warp CPU relative velocity difference \WlhCpuParityDiff; CUDA
parity \texttt{\WlhCudaParityStatus}). Timeouts and the comparison with the
fixed-fraction screening are reported, never gated, and the magnetic-moment variation
is \texttt{\WlhMuRole} (per-design medians from \WlhMuMedianMin{} to \WlhMuMedianMax,
largest single value \WlhMuMaxMax).

The protocol was frozen at preregistration commit \texttt{\WlhPreregCommit} after a
disclosed non-evidentiary shakedown on \WlhShakedownDesigns{} real designs at
\WlhShakedownBlock{} launches per cell block (\WlhShakedownCases{} cases,
\WlhShakedownValidators{} validator calls; disjoint from the evidentiary design:
\WlhShakedownDisjoint; passed: \WlhShakedownPassed; evidentiary:
\WlhShakedownEvidentiary), whose timing projection under the planning assumption of
\WlhPlanningToppedFraction{} topped-up cells (\WlhTimingProjectedMin~min against a budget
of \WlhTimingBudgetMin~min; within budget: \WlhTimingWithinBudget) admitted the
campaign while the worst case of every cell topped up (\WlhTimingWorstMin~min) did not
(\WlhTimingWorstWithinBudget). It was executed exactly \WlhAttemptCount{} time under an
immutable Git-common lock (immutable: \WlhLockImmutable) on \WlhWorkerPool{} of
\WlhCpuCount{} CPU cores (\texttt{\WlhDevice}; lock to terminal \WlhExecutionWallMin~min,
case pool \WlhCasesWallMin~min). The results are committed at
\texttt{\WlhResultsCommit} (manifest hash prefix \texttt{\WlhManifestSha},
\WlhVerifiedFiles{} files verified byte for byte, \WlhToleratedEolFiles{} accepted
through any end-of-line tolerance; orbit\_mc source \texttt{\WlhOrbitMcSourceSha}, field
pipeline \texttt{\WlhFieldPipelineSha} over \WlhFieldPipelineFiles{} files, protocol
\texttt{\WlhProtocolSha}, experiment code \texttt{\WlhExperimentCodeSha} over
\WlhExperimentCodeFiles{} files), the disclosure of the post-hoc manifest publication
and the runtime fix at \texttt{\WlhDisclosureCommit}, and the results dashboard at
\texttt{\WlhDashboardCommit}, whose embedded extraction the evidence generator requires
to agree with the sealed artifacts.
